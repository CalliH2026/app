# Moodle 4 (assignment activity): entering feedback comments for a student's assignment submission as a teacher (grading page)

> **Draft from official documentation, not yet verified against the application.**

## Procedure

1. The cited Moodle 4 documentation supports this basic workflow, but it does **not** confirm an exact confirmation message, autosave behavior, or general validation steps for the individual grading page. The exact screen layout and labels can also vary with assignment settings.
2. Open the assignment from the course page.
3. On its summary page, click **Grade** to open a student’s grading page. To choose a particular student, click **View all submissions**, then use that student’s grading link in the table.
4. Enter the comment in **Feedback comments**. This field appears when feedback comments are enabled.
5. Click **Save changes** to save, or **Save and show next** to save and open the next submission. In the table, the documentation describes links to grade a submission and columns for feedback comments and files when enabled. It does not establish that **Grade**, **Feedback comments**, or **Edit** are the exact names of every relevant column or control in all Moodle 4 configurations. **On save:** The documentation confirms the save buttons and, for **Save and show next**, moving to the next submission. It does not specify the individual grading page’s confirmation message, whether it autosaves, or a general validation step. The rubric documentation separately says that each criterion must have a level selected for the rubric to be validated; that requirement applies when grading with a rubric.
