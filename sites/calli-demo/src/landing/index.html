Clear about what
Calli does.

A prototype with visible sources and visible limits.

Do I have to approve every dictated comment?

No. A completed dictation is queued automatically after identity and context checks. The agent writes it and reads it back. STOP prevents new work; an action already in progress may finish.

Does Calli watch everything on my computer?

No. This prototype reacts to documents opened inside Calli. General desktop observation and simultaneous independent agents are future work.

Is the time saving measured?

The native execution times are measured. Human time savings are estimates. For ten already-written comments, a stated typing-versus-dictation model gives about 49% less active entry time. There is no timed human baseline yet.

Can a school use this in production today?

This is a hackathon prototype using fictional students. The parent view is a local demonstration, not a production account or access-control system. No French-hosting or regulatory certification claim is made.
