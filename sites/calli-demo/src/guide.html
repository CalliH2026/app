Explore Calli in five minutes.

This is the New Pronote workspace with Calli, running on saved outputs from real recorded runs. Students, parents and documents are fictional. Nothing you click here starts an agent, calls a provider or writes to PRONOTE.

Try the workspace →

What to try

  1. Today. Mme Laurent’s day: the parent meeting about Alex Martin’s Pythagoras exercise.
  2. The copy and its sources. Open Évaluations or Documents: Alex’s copy, the marking scheme, the instructions and the method sheet.
  3. The saved H preparation report. Open the report: evidence linked to the copy, the parent voice note transcript, sourced resources and the H tool trace. Opening the copy in the workspace also shows this saved report instead of starting a new run.
  4. The interactive exercise. In the report, the practice card shows the exercise generated through the Devin API. Open it on its own and try a right answer, a wrong answer and reset.
  5. The school trip. Open the saved research draft (Musée d’Orsay, 4e B): sources, budget lines and the tool trace. It is a partial draft; nothing is booked or paid.
  6. The family view. Grégory Martin’s page: a short summary for Alex Martin and Lucas Martin, with each item linked to its source.
  7. Appreciations. The report-card comments for the fictional 3A class, with portraits. The native PRONOTE write and read-back are shown in the recorded demo video.

Where this comes from

The workspace HTML, CSS and JavaScript are the product files of Calli UI commit a0d922e. The data is the product’s own read-only API output, exported from a local offline server with saved demo data. Details are in the package README.
